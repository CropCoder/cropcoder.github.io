Bioinformatics researcher working at the intersection of crop disease resistance, wheat genomics, and computational biology. My research focuses on NLR pan-genome analysis, GWAS for stripe rust resistance loci, and the development of the LWRR knowledge platform, with a growing emphasis on machine learning and AI-driven discovery in plant immunity. I am also the author of 180+ bioinformatics tutorials that have reached over 300K readers. My goal is to translate population genomics and multi-omics evidence into practical tools for disease resistance breeding.
