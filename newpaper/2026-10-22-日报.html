摘要 · 逐句中英对照

Shoot architecture, mainly determined by tiller angle, is a key agronomic trait affecting crop yield.

地上部株型主要由分蘖角决定，是影响作物产量的关键农艺性状。

Winter wheat undergoes dynamic erect-prostrate growth transitions in response to low temperatures during overwintering, yet the coordinated regulation of shoot architecture by temperature and gravity remains elusive.

冬小麦在越冬期响应低温而发生动态的直立–匍匐生长转变，然而温度与重力如何协同调控株型此前不清楚。

In this study, we identified TaHGH1, an HSFA2 transcription factor, via GWAS and found it positively regulates shoot gravitropism and thereby reduces tiller angle.

本研究通过 GWAS 鉴定出 TaHGH1——一个 HSFA2 类转录因子，发现它正向调控地上部向重力性，从而减小分蘖角。

TaHGH1 promotes amyloplast abundance to enhance gravitropism; heat-induced transcription and cold-induced alternative splicing of TaHGH1 jointly modulate gravitropism and the erect-prostrate-erect growth shift.

TaHGH1 通过促进淀粉体丰度增强向重力性；热诱导的转录与冷诱导的可变剪接共同调控向重力性与「直立–匍匐–直立」的生长转变。

Mechanistically, TaHGH1 directly activates TaLAX3 and TaARF19, leading to asymmetric auxin distribution and differential cell elongation.

机制上，TaHGH1 直接激活 TaLAX3 与 TaARF19，导致生长素不对称分布与差异细胞伸长。

与紧凑株型和小穗数增加相关的有利 TaHGH1 单倍型，已经历全球性的人工选择。

Our work reveals a molecular module integrating thermal and gravity signals for adaptive shoot architecture, providing valuable targets for high-yield wheat breeding.

本工作揭示了一个整合温度与重力信号以适应株型的分子模块，为高产小麦育种提供了有价值的靶点。

本文提出 GLRS——结合「GWAS 信号排序 + LD 剪枝 + 随机森林特征重要性筛选」的性状信息驱动框架，用于为小麦纯系与杂交 GS 育种推导超稀疏标记集。

摘要 · 逐句中英对照

Abscisic acid (ABA) was first identified in plants over six decades ago and is now recognized as one of the central phytohormones in plants, regulating growth, development, and physiological adaptation to environmental changes.

脱落酸（ABA）在六十多年前首次于植物中被鉴定，如今已被视为植物核心激素之一，调控生长、发育与对环境变化的生理适应。

In this Centennial Update, we use two influential ABA-related articles published in Plant Physiology in the early 2000s and discuss complementary advances in stress-responsive transcription and ABA biosynthetic regulation.

在本篇百年综述中，我们以 2000 年代初发表于 Plant Physiology 的两篇有影响力的 ABA 相关文章为线索，讨论胁迫响应转录与 ABA 生物合成调控方面的互补进展。

We extend the Update from ABA-relevant gene discovery to recent advances in multicellular coordination of stomatal control.

我们把综述从 ABA 相关基因发掘延伸到气孔调控的多细胞协调这一近期进展。

Growing evidence supports the view that ABA-regulated stomatal movement and gas exchange are coordinated across multiple cell types, including guard cells, mesophyll cells, bundle sheath cells, and vascular tissues in roots and leaves.

越来越多的证据支持这一观点：ABA 调控的气孔运动与气体交换在多种细胞类型间协调，包括保卫细胞、叶肉细胞、维管束鞘细胞以及根与叶的维管组织。

We also highlight emerging tools to dissect this ABA-mediated multicellular network by using emerging technologies in single-cell and spatial omics, plant optogenetics, and artificial intelligence (AI).

我们还重点介绍了用于解析这一 ABA 介导多细胞网络的新兴工具：单细胞与空间组学、植物光遗传学以及人工智能（AI）。

These advances will shape ABA research from single cell models toward multicellular collaborative networks for understanding plant-environment interactions.

这些进展将推动 ABA 研究从单细胞模型走向多细胞协同网络，以理解植物–环境互作。

Together, this article links the history of ABA research with emerging single-cell resolution datasets and AI-assisted computational approaches, proposing a framework for moving ABA biology from well-established guard-cell signaling models toward a broader understanding of multicellular ABA coordination.

综上，本文把 ABA 研究的历史与新兴的单细胞分辨率数据集、AI 辅助计算方法联系起来，提出一个框架：将 ABA 生物学从成熟的保卫细胞信号模型推向对多细胞 ABA 协调的更广义理解。

摘要 · 逐句中英对照

Nitrogen availability is one of the main factors limiting crop productivity, and the low nitrogen use efficiency (NUE) of crops imposes significant economic and environmental costs.

氮素可获得性是限制作物生产力的主要因素之一，而作物较低的氮利用效率（NUE）带来显著的经济与环境成本。

Research on NUE has traditionally focused on improving nitrogen uptake and assimilation; however, the efficient redistribution of assimilated nitrogen within the plant is equally important for growth, yield, and crop quality.

NUE 研究传统上聚焦于改善氮的吸收与同化；然而，已同化氮在植株体内的有效再分配对生长、产量与作物品质同等重要。

Amino acids are the main form in which reduced nitrogen is transported over long distances.

氨基酸是还原态氮进行长距离运输的主要形式。

Their movement between source and sink tissues depends on a diverse group of membrane transporters belonging to the Amino Acid/Auxin Permeases (AAAP), Amino Acid/Polyamine/Choline transporters (APC), and Usually Multiple Acids Move In and Out Transporters (UMAMIT) families.

它们在源与库组织之间的移动依赖于一类多样的膜转运蛋白，分别属于 AAAP、APC 与 UMAMIT 家族。

These transporters mediate amino acid uptake, export, vascular loading and unloading, intracellular transport, and redistribution to developing organs, thereby influencing plant growth, reproductive development and the composition of harvested products.

这些转运蛋白介导氨基酸的吸收、外排、维管装载与卸载、胞内运输以及向发育器官的再分配，从而影响植物生长、生殖发育与收获产品的组成。

Recent work in cereals, legumes and horticultural crops has shown that modifying amino acid transport can alter biomass production, grain filling, seed protein content, fruit quality, and NUE.

近期在禾谷类、豆类与园艺作物中的研究表明，改造氨基酸运输可改变生物量生产、籽粒灌浆、种子蛋白含量、果实品质与氮利用效率。

In this review, we summarize current knowledge of plant amino acid transporters, from their molecular and physiological functions to their roles in crop yield, nutritional quality and NUE.

本综述系统总结了植物氨基酸转运蛋白的现有认识，从分子与生理功能到其在作物产量、营养品质与 NUE 中的作用。

We also discuss how advances in genome editing, exploitation of natural genetic variation, high-throughput phenotyping and systems biology may facilitate the development of crops with more efficient nitrogen use and improved agricultural sustainability.

我们还讨论了基因组编辑、自然遗传变异挖掘、高通量表型与系统生物学的进展如何促进培育氮利用更高效、农业更可持续的作物。

摘要 · 逐句中英对照

Clubroot disease, caused by the obligate biotrophic protist Plasmodiophora brassicae, threatens Brassica crops worldwide.

根肿病由专性活体营养型原生生物根肿病菌（Plasmodiophora brassicae）引起，威胁全球十字花科作物。

Novel pathogen virulence frequently breaks resistance in agricultural crops, and yet explanations for isolate evolution remain unknown.

新的病原毒力常突破作物抗性，然而对菌株演化的解释仍然未知。

We combined whole genome sequencing of 192 isolates from 24 countries with a pangenome analysis of 55 high–quality complete or nearly complete genome assemblies and performed functional effector analyses to investigate potential mechanisms of virulence emergence.

我们整合了来自 24 个国家的 192 个菌株的全基因组测序，与 55 个高质量完整或近完整基因组组装的泛基因组分析，并开展功能性效应子分析，以探究毒力涌现的潜在机制。

Three major phylogenetic clusters were identified, including two highly clonal lineages with contrasting virulence profiles, and a genetically diverse population retaining signatures of recombination.

鉴定出 3 个主要系统发育簇：包括两个毒力谱相反的高度克隆谱系，以及一个保留重组特征的遗传多样性群体。

Our findings support a model in which genome–wide purifying selection maintained core genome integrity, while effector genes evolved under less constraint.

我们的发现支持这样一个模型：全基因组的纯化选择维持核心基因组的完整性，而效应子基因在较小的约束下演化。

Despite a compact closed pangenome, we identified chromosome 17 as a major hotspot of single nucleotide and structural variations, transposable elements and expansion of ankyrin repeat effectors.

尽管泛基因组紧凑且闭合，我们发现 17 号染色体是单核苷酸与结构变异、转座元件以及锚蛋白重复效应子扩张的主要热点。

Within the variable chromosome 17, PbAnk2 alleles from virulent and avirulent isolates encoded proteins with distinct predicted structures.

在变异的 17 号染色体内，来自强毒与弱毒菌株的 PbAnk2 等位基因编码的蛋白具有不同的预测结构。

PbAnk2 interacted with the Arabidopsis co–chaperone DJC31 in yeast two–hybrid and BiFC assays, whereas co–immunoprecipitation did not detect an interaction for the allele found in isolates that break CRa–mediated resistance, suggesting that allelic variation may alter PbAnk2′s association with DJC31.

在酵母双杂交与 BiFC 实验中，PbAnk2 与拟南芥共伴侣蛋白 DJC31 互作；然而对于突破 CRa 介导抗性的菌株中所含的等位基因，共免疫沉淀未能检测到该互作，提示等位变异可能改变 PbAnk2 与 DJC31 的结合。

These findings reveal the clonal expansion of virulent isolates and connect it to effector diversification that led to resistance breakdown and establish a genomic framework for pathogen surveillance and durable clubroot resistance.

这些发现揭示了强毒菌株的克隆扩张，并将其与导致抗性丧失的效应子多样化联系起来，为病原监测与持久根肿病抗性建立了基因组框架。

检索与核实说明

口径 · 方法 · 局限

1 · 时间窗口与「当天发表」的实际含义

出刊日为 2026-10-22（Asia/Shanghai）。你的需求是检索「当天发表」的论文，但实测：出刊日前 24 小时内，A/B/C 三层来源中没有任何一篇的实际首发日等于出刊日。

原因：Europe PMC 对最近 1–4 天的文献收录不完整，新论文从上线到入库通常滞后数天。这与工作区《论文周报/配置.md》第 5 节记录的实测经验一致。

本简报口径：回看窗口设为 5 天（2026-10-21 → 2026-10-22），取该窗口内全部可索引到的、落在 12 个方向上的文献；每篇均标注真实首发日（卡片与速览表内），供你自行判断新鲜度。因此本日篇数是下界而非全量，且必然与次日/次轮出刊存在窗口重叠。

2 · 来源分层（A / B / C）

A 层（顶刊白名单，2 篇）：Nature、Science、Cell、Nature Genetics、Nature Plants、Nature Communications、Science Advances、Molecular Plant、Nature Methods、Cell Host &amp; Microbe。

B 层（专业期刊，2 篇）：The Plant Cell、Plant Journal、Journal of Experimental Botany、New Phytologist、Plant Biotechnology Journal、Theoretical and Applied Genetics、Genome Biology、Horticulture Research、Plant Physiology、Plant Communications、Molecular Plant Pathology、Plant Cell Reports、Plant Science 等。

C 层（预印本，3 条）：bioRxiv、Research Square、Preprints.org。未经同行评审，单独放入「延伸阅读」，不与主榜同等看待。

分层依据为期刊名映射——Europe PMC 返回的是缩写刊名（如 Mol Plant），必须先归一化再判定层级，映射表见 fetch_daily.py 的 JOURNAL_MAP。

3 · 检索通道与可复现流程

主通道：Europe PMC REST API（resultType=core 取摘要原文）。按「期刊白名单 × 植物词 × 日期」与「方向检索式 × 期刊 × 日期」两轮交叉检索，再对预印本单独检索（SRC:&quot;PPR&quot;）。

作者与通讯作者：Europe PMC 的 authorList 不提供通讯作者字段，且对本批论文的 affiliation 为空；Crossref 的 sequence 也只区分 first/additional，不是通讯标记。因此通讯作者改用 NCBI PubMed XML 中 Electronic address: &lt;email&gt; 这一出版商官方标记判定；无该标记者回退「末位作者」惯例并明确标注为推断。姓名与单位另由 Crossref 补齐。

可复现流程：fetch_daily.py（检索）→ classify.py（方向分类，含 13 例回归测试）→ fetch_authors.py + fetch_corresponding.py（作者）→ merge_authors.py → assemble.py → build_report.py（生成 HTML）→ selfcheck.py（自检）。

4 · 已知局限与未核实项

  • 索引延迟：最近 1–4 天收录不完整，本日篇数为下界。
  • 方向分类为关键词规则（classify.py），已用 13 个真实样本做回归测试，并设置多层语境门（d1 须有基因层语境、d4 须有病害专有词或分子免疫证据、d5 须有免疫语境）以压制误标；但仍可能有漏标或误标，卡片内附「证据词」字段，便于复核判断依据。
  • 通讯作者：标 通讯作者 者依据 PubMed 官方 Electronic address 标记或期刊全文 corresp 字段；标（推断）者仅据末位作者惯例，未经官方确认。Wiley 系期刊部分文章网页被 Cloudflare 拦截，无法二次核对是否另有共同通讯。
  • 作者「主要研究方向」：仅在有明确公开来源时填写，并标注 confidence（confirmed / inferred / unconfirmed）；标 unconfirmed 者写明「未查到可靠公开信息」。未展示任何年龄信息（无可靠公开来源，且按你的要求省略）。
